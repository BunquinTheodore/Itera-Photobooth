About Itera

Why Itera

An elevated photobooth experience, now open inside BlueMoon Alangilan.

Itera Collectives began with a simple wish: that a photobooth could feel as special as the moment it captures. Our booth now stands inside BlueMoon Alangilan in Batangas City, ready to turn your laughter, your poses and your in between glances into keepsakes you can hold.

Every frame is a small celebration, and you are the guest of honor.

Step behind the curtain and the booth does the rest. A DSLR camera, a triple light system and an 18.5 inch OLED touchscreen keep every shot sharp, glowing and easy to take, while fast printing puts a fresh strip in your hands before the giggles fade.

An attendant is always on site, so you can simply step in, strike a pose and enjoy the moment.

  • DSLR camera setup

    Crisp, true to life pictures, every single time.

  • Triple light system

    Soft, flattering light from every angle.

  • 18.5 inch OLED touchscreen

    Tap, smile and shoot. It feels effortless.

  • Fast, effortless printing

    Your strip arrives warm, smooth and ready to keep.

FAQ

Good to know

A few quick answers before you book.

How do I book Itera's photobooth for my private event?

Simply send us a message on any of our social media pages with your event date, location and event type. We recommend booking as early as possible to make sure your date is available.

What type of events or bookings does Itera cater to?

Birthdays, debuts, weddings, school functions and festivals, corporate gatherings and team buildings, product launches, pop ups and private events. Whatever you are celebrating, we will bring the fun to your event.

What is included if we book your photobooth?

We provide unlimited prints for the booked duration, 2x6 or 4x6 cut formats for your prints, up to two personalized templates and frames to suit your event, and digital copies.

Do you require a down payment to reserve our date?

Yes, a 50% down payment is required to secure your event date and time. Your booking is confirmed once our team has received the payment.
